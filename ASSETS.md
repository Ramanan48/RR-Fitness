# Bundled assets — origins and licensing

Everything ships inside the APK. Nothing is streamed or downloaded at runtime.

## Voice clips — packed in `app/src/main/assets/voice.pack` (+ `voice.json` index)

22 clips per language (44 total, ≈ 0.64 MB), mono 24 kHz Ogg Vorbis, silence-trimmed,
loudness-normalised to −16 LUFS. Generated by `tools/make_voice.py`, then packed back-to-back
into one uncompressed file by `tools/pack_voice.py` (keeps the project under 100 files for
GitHub web upload); the app loads each clip with SoundPool at its byte offset.

"breathe"), which ends long before the next countdown.

- **English voice:** Kokoro-82M TTS (Apache-2.0, hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) via `kokoro-onnx` (MIT),
  blend of 70 % `af_heart` + 30 % `af_nicole` (warm adult female).
- **Tamil voice:** AI4Bharat Indic-TTS Tamil FastPitch + HiFi-GAN, `female` speaker
  (MIT licence, github.com/AI4Bharat/Indic-TTS, release v1-checkpoints-release), run with
  Coqui TTS (`coqui-tts`, MPL-2.0) on CPU. Tone is the dataset speaker's natural voice —
  clear and neutral rather than "husky".
- **Not human recordings, and not yet listened to by a person.** Lengths and loudness were
  measured automatically; speech content could not be machine-transcribed in the build
  environment. Please listen to both languages ("Test voice" on the home screen).
- The app never uses Android TTS or any online speech service.

## How-to videos — `app/src/main/res/raw/howto_<id>_<en|ta>.mp4`

20 videos (10 exercises × English/Tamil), 720×1280 portrait, H.264 Main + AAC mono, 30 fps,
fast-start. Made by `tools/make_howto_videos.py` from the same coach renderer and
animation data as the app, captions from `Exercises.kt`, narration from the voices above.
Structure: title → each step in slow motion (0.5×, matching part of the movement) →
full speed → form tips → no-jump/easier option → "Ready? Let's go!".
Tamil captions rendered with **Noto Sans Tamil** (SIL Open Font Licence) using HarfBuzz/raqm
shaping; the font is used only to make the videos and is not bundled in the app (Android's
own Tamil font shows the in-app text).

## Fitness coach (default guide style)

- **Origin:** original 2D cartoon coach drawn entirely from shapes in code, styled after the
  owner's preferred look: light-brown hair in a high ponytail, teal sports bra, teal leggings
  with a yellow side stripe, white sneakers, dark wristband, athletic build. It is a
  cartoon, not a realistic AI video.
- **Code:** `app/.../anim/Avatar.kt` (app) and `tools/avatar.py` (identical reference renderer
  used for previews and videos).
- **Switch:** Home → "Guide style" (coach ⇄ stick figure). Saved locally.
- **Preview:** `docs/avatar_exercises.png` (GIFs can be regenerated with `tools/avatar.py`).

## Coach photo — `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/coach_photo.jpg`

- Shown on the Home screen "Your Coach" card (with a daily motivation line in English or
  Tamil) and on the Completed screen.
- **Origin:** an AI-generated image supplied by the app owner (414×740, re-saved as JPEG).
  Its creator and licence are unknown. Fine for a personal, sideloaded app; **keep the
  GitHub repository private** and do not publish the app with this image unless you have
  the rights to it. To change it, replace this file (same name).

## Exercise animations — `app/src/main/assets/poses/poses.json`

Original joint-angle keyframes (`tools/make_poses.py`, with jump lift and sideways travel),
baked to 48–96 frames per loop. Licence: same as this project.

| # | Exercise | Level | Jump? | No-jump option | View | Loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | March with arm swings | Warm-up | no | — | side | 1.6 s |
| 2 | Step jacks | Warm-up | no | — | front | 2.0 s |
| 3 | High knees | Beginner | light | fast march | side | 0.9 s |
| 4 | Butt kicks | Beginner | light | walk-pace heel curls | side | 0.9 s |
| 5 | Shadow boxing | Beginner | light bounce | slower punches | side | 1.2 s |
| 6 | Jumping jacks | Advanced | yes | step jacks | front | 1.2 s |
| 7 | Skaters | Advanced | yes | side step + back tap | front | 1.6 s |
| 8 | Mountain climbers | Advanced | no (floor) | hands on chair/bed | side | 0.9 s |

All 10 guides exist. Alternating moves show both sides.
Notes: high knees and butt kicks are drawn without a flight phase; mountain climbers use a
slightly inclined plank so the hands reach the floor in a 2D side view.
